# Template de script en 9 temps

Une phrase par ligne. Les passages entre `[ ]` sont à remplacer ; les phrases sans crochets sont la mécanique observée sur la chaîne (`analyses-par-video.md`), à garder ou à paraphraser une fois. Budgets de mots dans le `SKILL.md`, section 2 (12 min ≈ 2 000 mots).

## TEMPS 1 — LE GESTE (70–90 mots)

```
Try [GESTE EN 3 OU 4 ACTIONS ENCHAÎNÉES : getting up right now, closing your door, turning the key in the lock twice, and turning off the light].
Listen closely to [LE SON DU GESTE] and feel [LA SENSATION QUI SUIT].
In this moment, you have done something almost impossible.
[CE QUE LE GESTE ACCOMPLIT, EN IMAGE : You have wiped out the entire world with a flick of your hand.]
[2 OU 3 PHRASES DE 4 À 7 MOTS : No eyes are watching you. No ears are hearing how you breathe.]
```
Règles : impératif, présent, 2ᵉ personne ; un objet que le spectateur a sous la main ; aucune abstraction, aucun chiffre.

## TEMPS 2 — RETIRE-LE + PROMESSE (110–140 mots)

```
We do this every day without a second thought, treating [SUJET] as a natural right we were born with.
But if we go back in time.
And remove [ÉLÉMENT 1 : these four walls].
And remove [ÉLÉMENT 2 : the door and the key].
You will be shocked by a terrifying truth.
[LA VÉRITÉ EN UNE PHRASE AVEC UN CHIFFRE : Throughout most of human history, not a single person on Earth knew what it meant to be alone.]
For more than [200,000] years, [SUJET] was not a luxury.
[SUJET] meant.
[UN MOT : Death.]
So, [PREMIÈRE QUESTION DU TITRE, MOT POUR MOT] ?
[DEUXIÈME QUESTION DU TITRE, MOT POUR MOT] ?
```

## TEMPS 3 — LA TRIBU (200–240 mots)

```
To understand the scale of this nightmare, you have to imagine living in a tribe [200,000] years ago.
You are one member of a group of [20 to 50] people moving together through [LIEU].
[3 PHRASES EN ANAPHORE SUR LE CORPS : You wake up with people around you, you eat with people around you. You fight, cry, and rejoice in front of those same 20 people.]
There is no such thing as [CE QUI MANQUE : "going home"].
[LA NUIT OU LE DANGER : Night in prehistoric times was a nightmare that terrified our ancestors, and it forced them to huddle together around the fire.]
Meaning, at the time you most needed [X], you had to [SON CONTRAIRE].
That is why ancient humans had to invent the first [RÉPONSE] in history.
```

## TEMPS 4 — CHAPITRES 1 À 3 : LA PREMIÈRE RÉPONSE (500–560 mots, ≈ 180 par chapitre)

Chaque chapitre suit la même mécanique :

```
[OUVERTURE : But this [X] wasn't a wall you built out of bricks. / Anthropologists, when they studied the tribes still living today in the Amazon and Africa, discovered something amazing.]
[LE MÉCANISME EN 3 OU 4 PHRASES CONCRÈTES AVEC UN CHIFFRE : All you can do is walk 30 steps. And stand behind any tree or rock.]
[LA RÈGLE SOCIALE OU LE DÉTAIL SURPRENANT : the entire tribe makes a silent, unwritten agreement that no one looks at you.]
[L'APHORISME : Privacy wasn't a room. It was a small distance and a terrifying respect for these imaginary rules.]
[POURQUOI, EN UNE PHRASE DE SURVIE : falling apart in those eras meant certain death for everyone.]
[LA TRANSITION : This plan worked perfectly, until… / And this takes us to… / But here, the puzzle becomes more complex.]
```

## TEMPS 5 — LE PIÈGE (120–160 mots, vers 50 %)

```
[LE CONFORT QUI TRAHIT : Humans created the worst invention in the history of [SUJET] : the invention of walls and houses.]
We always think that [CONFORT] brought [SUJET], but the truth is the exact opposite.
Come on. Let's travel to [LIEU DATÉ : a city called Çatalhöyük in Turkey about 9,000 years ago].
[4 OU 5 PHRASES DE DÉTAILS PHYSIQUES GÊNANTS : no streets, you walk over your neighbors' roofs, smoke, everyone sleeps and changes clothes in the same room, the dead under the floor.]
[LA PHRASE-CHOC COURTE : You were trapped with your family in a mud box.]
```

## TEMPS 6 — CHAPITRES 4 À 6 : LA CHAÎNE DES CIVILISATIONS (560–640 mots)

Une civilisation par chapitre, dans l'ordre chronologique, chacune avec : un lieu ou un mot daté, le privilège des riches, un détail cruel ou gênant, un aphorisme.

```
And this takes us to the most shocking part of our history : [CIVILISATION ET PRATIQUE].
[LE DÉTAIL CONCRET EN 3 PHRASES : The Romans had public toilets called foricae. Long stone benches with holes carved side by side. No dividers, no stalls, no curtains.]
You, a respectable Roman citizen, [LE SPECTATEUR DANS LA SCÈNE].
[L'APHORISME : [SUJET] at that time wasn't a human right. It was a very expensive privilege.]
[LA VERSION DES PUISSANTS : The Romans built private toilets inside their palaces.]
```
Puis, au choix selon le sujet : l'Égypte (la face publique et la face privée, la tombe), la Grèce (un mot qui a changé de sens : « idiotes »), Babylone (une loi et sa punition), les palais européens (l'enfilade, « the bed itself was the bedroom »). Un chapitre peut porter sur l'esprit (Cicéron et ses lettres) quand le sujet s'y prête.

## TEMPS 7 — L'INVENTION (130–160 mots)

```
Until a simple [architectural / culinary / medical] invention happened, but it changed the face of humanity and its psyche forever.
That is [L'INVENTION EN UN MOT : the hallway].
[LE MÉCANISME EN 3 PHRASES : Engineers finally started designing a long corridor, and all the rooms opened onto it independently. For the first time, you could enter your room and lock your door.]
And here, an urgent need for another great invention appeared : [LA SECONDE INVENTION : the lock with a key].
[CE QUE LES DEUX RÉUNIES SIGNIFIENT : our personal space, our secrets, and our moments of weakness are also a very precious treasure.]
```

## TEMPS 8 — LE TWIST MODERNE (120–150 mots)

```
We fought for thousands of years, [RÉSUMÉ EN UNE PHRASE DE LA CHAÎNE], to reach a very simple moment in the end.
[LE GESTE, REDIT EN UNE PHRASE.]
But the irony is that after all these years, [LE RETOURNEMENT : we are the ones who decided to destroy everything we built with our own hands.]
[L'OBJET MODERNE : But you are holding a glowing piece of glass in your hand.]
[3 PHRASES SUR CE QU'IL FAIT : This screen brought the whole world inside your bed. Algorithms now know what you are thinking about in the dead of night.]
[LA PHRASE-MIROIR : We returned again to the era of the primitive tribe, where everyone sees everyone.]
```
Variante positive (sucre, maladies) : « You are not weak and your body is not broken. » / « You are the winning version after 50,000 years of war. »

## TEMPS 9 — RETOUR AU GESTE (70–90 mots)

```
That's why tonight, when you [LE GESTE DU TEMPS 1, MOT POUR MOT].
Stand for just one second in [LE LIEU].
And listen to [LE SON].
And remember that [LE SON] is not just [SA VERSION BANALE].
It is the sound of a great human victory.
It took us [200,000] years of agony to reach it.
[DERNIÈRE PHRASE : une question au spectateur (Has this phone left you any real privacy that's worth locking the door over?) ou un aphorisme (The treasure that used to cost lives is now sitting right there on the table.)]
```

## Vérification par code

`python scripts/yt/script_metrics.py script.md --minutes 12` : mots par phrase 11 à 14 ; « you » 2 à 4 pour 100 mots ; nombres 1 à 2,5 par minute ; phrases ≤ 4 mots 3 à 10 % ; 0 « subscribe » ; 3 à 9 questions. Un écart = réécrire le temps concerné, pas ajouter des mots ailleurs.
